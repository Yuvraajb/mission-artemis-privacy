Mission Artemis

Privacy policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Mission Artemis does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens locally, on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, location, or any identifying information.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not create user accounts or require sign-in.
  • It does not transmit any data to our servers or to any third party, because we do not operate any servers that the app communicates with.
  • It does not share data with third parties, because none is collected in the first place.

Crew AI chat

The app includes a feature that lets you have conversations with the Artemis II crew members. These conversations are powered by an on-device AI model that runs entirely on your iPad. Nothing you type, and nothing the model generates, ever leaves your device or is sent to us or anyone else.

Local settings

The app saves your accessibility and display preferences (such as Reduce Motion, High Contrast, Larger Text, and Color Blind Mode) locally on your device so they persist between sessions. This information stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Children's privacy

Mission Artemis does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data collection), we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through the support page linked in the app's App Store listing.
